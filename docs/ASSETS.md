### Use in this project

The original chair geometry is used only as a demonstration asset inside the
Interactive Sales Kit. At runtime, the application targets the model material
named `Mesh` so the seat and back mesh can visually follow the fictional
Graphite, Slate, and Sand sales variants.

The project does not claim that the original creator designed or endorsed the
fictional ERGO N1 product.

**NUSAKARYA, ERGO N1, all prices, specifications, product benefits, variants,
sales content, and business information shown in this sample are fictional and
exist only for demonstration purposes.**
